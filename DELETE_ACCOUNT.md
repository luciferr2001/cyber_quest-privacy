# Delete your CyberQuest account and data

You can delete your CyberQuest account at any time.

## In the app
1. Open CyberQuest and sign in.
2. Tap your profile picture, then **Profile and settings**.
3. Choose **Delete account** and confirm. You may be asked to sign in again to confirm it is you.

## Without the app
Email **rathodrutik05@gmail.com** from the address linked to your Google account with the subject "Delete my CyberQuest account". Include the name shown on your profile. We will delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

## What is deleted
Your sign-in record (name, email, profile photo link, user ID) and all learning progress stored in our database: XP, level, streak, completed and attempted missions, quiz history and settings.

## What may remain
Anonymous analytics and crash reports that are not linked to your identity may be kept in aggregate for up to 14 months. Ad-related data collected by Google AdMob is handled by Google under its own policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.
