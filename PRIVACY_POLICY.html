CyberQuest Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

CyberQuest ("the app") is a cyber security learning app made by Rutik Rathod. This policy explains what the app collects and why. Contact: rathodrutik05@gmail.com.

Who the app is for

CyberQuest is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

What we collect

  • Account data (optional). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile photo and a unique user ID from Google. You can also use the app as a guest, which creates an anonymous user ID with no name or email.
  • Learning progress. XP, level, streak, completed and attempted missions, quiz results and settings (for example theme and reminder preferences). These are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) under your user ID so progress follows you across devices.
  • Usage and diagnostics. Firebase Analytics records which screens and features are used (for example a mission was opened or finished). Firebase Crashlytics records crash reports with device model, operating system version and app version. We do not log what you type in the Notes search box.
  • Advertising data. The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location derived from it, and ad interaction data, to serve and measure ads. Where required, we ask for your consent through Google's consent form, and you can change it in the app's privacy options.
  • Local data on your device. Some settings and quiz progress are stored on your device with SharedPreferences. Nothing in the app asks for your precise location, contacts, photos or microphone.

How we use it

To run the app, save your progress, show your name and level, send reminders you turn on, fix crashes, understand which features are useful, and show ads that keep the app free. We do not sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

Service providers that process data for us: Google Firebase (authentication, database, analytics, crash reporting) and Google AdMob (advertising). Their handling of data is described at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy. We do not share data with anyone else except if the law requires it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and access to each user's progress is restricted by database security rules to that user.

Retention and deletion

Progress data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account and all progress data at any time inside the app (Profile and settings, Delete account), or without installing the app by following the steps at https://luciferr2001.github.io/cyberquest-privacy/DELETEACCOUNT.html. Analytics and crash data that is no longer linked to an account may be kept in aggregate form for up to 14 months, as set in Firebase.

Your choices and rights

You can use the app as a guest, turn off reminders, change ad consent in the app's privacy options, reset your device advertising ID in Android settings, and ask for access to or deletion of your data by emailing the address above. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have additional rights, and we will honour valid requests.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change and, for material changes, the app will tell you.
